ABOUT ME

I am Kristene Airell A. Mendoza, a full-time 3rd year Multimedia Studies student. I am an artist and a writer so naturally, one of my main goals in life is to create something worth seeing. I am not creating just because it's something I want to do but because creating is important. I believe that creativity and inspiration are cycles that should never end just because someone says it will. I aspire to be a storyboard artist, a background artist, or a comic book artist. Maybe if I'm lucky and good enough in the future, I could be all three at once.
